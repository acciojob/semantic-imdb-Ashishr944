Summary

3 Idiots is a comedy-drama film about three engineering students, their friendship, college life, and their journey to follow their dreams instead of blindly following societal expectations.

Actors

The main actors are Aamir Khan as Rancho, R. Madhavan as Farhan, Sharman Joshi as Raju, Kareena Kapoor as Pia, and Boman Irani as Professor Viru Sahastrabuddhe.
